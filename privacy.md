# Flyer2Cal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Flyer2Cal (formerly Zettl) processes screenshots, OCR text, QR codes, calendar data, and saved events locally on the device. Flyer2Cal does not operate its own cloud backend and does not send screenshot content, OCR text, QR contents, calendar data, contacts, or partner details to a Flyer2Cal server.

## Advertising

Flyer2Cal may display non-personalized advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob and its service providers may process device information, device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad impressions, and interactions with advertisements to deliver, limit, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising. Flyer2Cal does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and does not request permission for cross-app tracking. Advertising consent choices are handled through Google's User Messaging Platform where required.

## Purchases

Flyer2Cal uses RevenueCat and Apple's StoreKit to process and restore the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. RevenueCat and Apple may process purchase, entitlement, app configuration, and anonymous app-user information needed to validate transactions and restore access. Flyer2Cal does not receive payment card or bank details.

## Local processing

Screenshot analysis, event extraction, OCR, and QR-code recognition are performed on the device. Screenshots and OCR results remain in the app's local storage unless the user explicitly shares content using the operating system's sharing features or creates a calendar/email/share action.

## Permissions

Flyer2Cal requests access to photos, camera, calendar, and contacts only when needed for the selected feature. Permission data and event records are managed by the operating system and stored locally by the app. The user can deny or revoke permissions in system settings.

## Sharing

When the user explicitly chooses to share an event, iOS or Android passes the selected content to the chosen system or third-party app. Flyer2Cal does not control that recipient's subsequent processing.

## Data retention and rights

Locally stored events, screenshots, and settings can be deleted by the user from the app or device. Requests concerning third-party services should be directed to the relevant provider.
